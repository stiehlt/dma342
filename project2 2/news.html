Community News

At The Daily Grind Café, we value the idea of traditional coffee shops being a social hub and a place for conversation. Deeply rooted in our community, we offer 3 community events where people can choose to express themselves, connect and share ideas, explore new perspectives, and build meaningful relationships.

sign up for our next café science. every week you meet a new individual ready to share their knowledge. from professors to entrepreneurs to scientists, you will learn something new, explore different perspectives, and engage in meaningful conversations.
